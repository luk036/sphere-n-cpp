Detailed Description

N-dimensional sphere sequence generator using cylindrical coordinates.

Recursively builds a point on S^(n-1) as P = (sqrt(1 - z^2) * P_{n-1}, z), where z = 2*VdC - 1 is in [-1, 1] and P_{n-1} comes from the lower-dimensional generator, with a Circle generator as the 2D base case. Thread-safe (internal mutex).
